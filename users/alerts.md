# Alerts

Alerts is the direction MansaFi reaches out to you: whatever is happening on the account, raised at the moment it matters, so checking manually is never the only way to find out.

---

## What fires

| Type | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Transfer | A payment you sent or received changed status — settled, failed |
| Policy | An agent's spend policy blocked something or wants your approval |
| Security | A sign-in, key change, or wallet change on the account |
| System | Platform notices unattached to any particular payment |

Each alert wears its type, so a glance is enough to tell action (Policy, usually) from information.

---

## Working the list

- Unread entries carry a filled dot and a lifted background
- Opening one marks it read
- **Mark all read** clears the lot
- The tabs at the top switch between **All** and **Unread**

---

## Preferences

Beneath the list, **Notification preferences** exposes every rule as an independent switch. Switching one off stops future alerts of that kind from being raised. It hides nothing already delivered, and it alters no behavior whatsoever.

That last point deserves emphasis: silencing a Policy alert does not relax your agent's spend policy by a cent. It only stops MansaFi from mentioning when the policy did its job. Enforcement runs whether or not anyone is watching it. [Policy Engine](../agents/policy-engine.md) covers how that enforcement actually works.
